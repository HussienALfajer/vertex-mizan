# apps/worker

NestJS 12 application context (no HTTP server) for background work with pg-boss (ADR 0002): month-end releases, revaluation previews, held-event retries, the reconciliation check, exports and PDFs, each with its spec. Copied from Vertex Food's `apps/worker` and adapted; logging follows Vertex Hub's worker. S01 added the email outbox and the daily clean-up; S03 the daily anchor and the full export.

## Layout
- `src/main.ts`: loads the config, creates the application context, uses the pino logger, enables shutdown hooks. pg-boss's timers keep the process alive.
- `src/worker.module.ts`: `WorkerModule.forRoot(config, jobs)`: config, nestjs-pino, the app and jobs roles' databases, the files, the email transport, the queue.
- `src/core/config/`: `loadConfig(env)`: `DATABASE_QUEUE_URL` must name `mizan_queue` and `DATABASE_APP_URL` `mizan_app` (a `user` query parameter is refused), `DATABASE_JOBS_URL` `mizan_jobs` (S03), `APP_URL` (the web app's origin, the base of emailed links, as in `apps/api`), `MIZAN_FILES_DIR` (as in `apps/api`: absolute, required in production, `.data/files/` otherwise), `SMTP_URL` (`smtp://` or `smtps://`; locally compose.yaml's Mailpit), `EMAIL_FROM`, `NODE_ENV`, `LOG_LEVEL`.
- `src/core/database/`: `DatabaseModule`, one pool as `mizan_app` (`DATABASE` token), as in `apps/api`, and one as `mizan_jobs` (`JOBS_DATABASE`), which reaches across companies only through the grants and policies `packages/db`'s convention test pins (S03).
- `src/core/files/`: `FileStore` (`FilesModule`, global): the files under `MIZAN_FILES_DIR` laid out as `apps/api`'s, written from a stream through a temporary name, read whole (null when gone) and removed (no error when gone). `src/core/clock/today.ts`: `todayInDamascus()`, `dayBefore()`.
- `src/core/email/`: `EmailModule` and `EMAIL_TRANSPORT`: the server's SMTP account through nodemailer; the tests put `CapturingTransport` in its place.
- `src/core/queue/`: `QueueModule` (pg-boss as `mizan_queue` in the `pgboss` schema with `createSchema: false`; at start it installs or migrates its tables, then creates (with the job's `queueOptions`), schedules and works every job, and queues once a job marked `runAtStart`; graceful stop), `ScheduledJob` and `QueueWorker` (S05: a queue another process sends to, worked job by job with its data, never scheduled).
- `src/jobs/<module>/<name>.job.ts`: one scheduled job per file, listed in `src/jobs/index.ts`. Pattern to copy: `src/jobs/system/heartbeat.job.ts` (`system.heartbeat`, every minute, logs `Heartbeat`). S01: `src/jobs/email/send.job.ts` (`email.send`, every minute: claims the due outbox rows with a 5-minute lease and `SKIP LOCKED`, sends them in queue order, deletes them with their token (a keyed email, S05's held digest, is kept with its `sent_at` instead); a failure keeps its code and waits 2, 4 … 60 minutes; pg-boss keeps its runs one day) with `templates.ts` (the Arabic text from `ar.email`, times in Damascus with Latin digits), and `src/jobs/system/clean-up.job.ts` (`system.clean-up`, daily at 02:00 UTC: expired or idle sessions, expired tokens, old rate-limit rows, keyed emails sent two days ago; expired invitations marked revoked with an audit row per invitation, per company in one transaction). S03, **the pattern for a job across companies**: `src/jobs/ledger/daily-anchor.job.ts` (`ledger.daily-anchor`, 21:30 UTC, 00:30 in Damascus: as the jobs role in one transaction under an advisory lock, each company's chain head at the end of the previous day into `chain_anchors` and one `daily_anchor` email per owner listing all their companies; the email goes in through the four outbox columns the jobs role holds, as SQL, since Drizzle would name every column; `anchor(day)` for the tests), `src/jobs/company/export.job.ts` (`company.export`, every minute: the jobs role finds the queued exports; each is claimed in its own transaction, its attempt counted so one that keeps killing the worker fails at the third (`EXPORT_ATTEMPTS`), the temporary files a killed build left removed (`FileStore.removePartials`); the app role builds it in its company from a repeatable-read snapshot under a skip-locked row lock, then `ready` for 24 hours with the owner emailed, or `failed` with its code and whatever file it wrote deleted) with `archive.ts` (`writeArchive`: streamed to its file as it is built, a table at a time and each attachment opened only when the ZIP reaches it (`addReadStreamLazy`) and read as it is written and checked against the digest recorded at upload (`ATTACHMENT_CHANGED`); every company table of the registry as JSON and CSV, values as PostgreSQL prints them so amounts stay integer strings, dates whatever the DateStyle; `manifest.json` with row counts, each file's SHA-256, the ledgers' currencies, the missing attachments and the chain head; the README in Arabic and English says which currency each amount column is in and that SYP is the new pound; through yazl; `csvField` disarms formulas), and `src/jobs/company/export-expire.job.ts` (`company.export-expire`, hourly: the files past their time deleted and the rows marked `expired`). S05, `src/jobs/events/`: `retry.job.ts` (`events.retry`, the queue the API sends to after a commit: the job parsed with contracts' `eventsRetryJobSchema`, `retryFreed()` in its company as the app role), `retry-sweep.job.ts` (`events.retry-sweep`, 01:00 UTC, 04:00 in Damascus, and at start: the jobs role finds the companies with held events through its policy, each retried with the `sweep` trigger in its own transaction), `held-digest.job.ts` (`events.held-digest`, 05:00 UTC, 08:00 in Damascus: one `held_digest` email per company, day and owner or accountant when an event is held, keyed in the outbox's `dedupe_key`, which outlasts the send, so its retry or a second run that day queues nothing; each company in its own transaction; S07: the line "أشهر منتهية غير مقفلة", each ended month not closed with its failing checklist items from `readPeriods()`; S08 rule 16: the jobs role lists every company and the email goes when any section has content, with "أيام المطابقة غير النظيفة" (the 31 latest days `missing` or `differences`, after the close marked) and the link to `/reconciliation`, now in Damascus from `nowInDamascus()`; `run(day, now)` for the tests) and `retries.ts` (`retryInCompany()`, nothing in an archived company; the sweep first maps the template's slots a later spec appended, `mapAppendedTemplateSlots()`). S06, `src/jobs/revenue/release-month.job.ts` (`revenue.release-month`, 22:00 UTC, 01:00 in Damascus, and at start: the jobs role lists the companies; each, as the app role in its context and one transaction, gets the template's recognition slots when it predates them, then every ended month that still misses its release (`releaseMissingMonths()`), a release that cannot post left missing and counted; `runMonth()`, the server command `revenue:run-release --month YYYY-MM` in `src/commands/run-release.ts`).
- S09: `src/core/print/` (`pdf.ts`: `printPdf()`, a whole HTML document printed on A4 by Chromium in its own context, JavaScript off and every request refused, "صفحة n من m" from the footer template or no footer for a document that numbers its own pages; `pdf-printer.ts`: `PDF_PRINTER` and `ChromiumPrinter` through `playwright-core` (the version the E2E image pins, `packages/config/test/ci.test.ts`), launched on the first print, two renders at a time, closed on shutdown; `PrintModule`, global), `src/jobs/reports/render.job.ts` (`reports.render`, the queue the API sends to after a file's row commits: the file claimed with its attempt counted (`RENDER_ATTEMPTS`), then read as the app role in one repeatable-read snapshot under a skip-locked lock with a 15-minute statement and idle limit, the report whole through `packages/db`'s `readReportAnswer()` or `readJournalBook()`, its sheet and HTML from `@vertex-mizan/ui/print` with the brand faces inlined, printed, written under its key and the row `ready` (24 hours, an official print kept); an official print refused `PERIOD_NOT_CLOSED` in the snapshot, else its register row (`readJournalBookTotals()`, the pages' first and last, `content_sha256` of the pages' RFC 8785 JSON) and `journal_book.printed` in the same transaction; a failure deletes its file and fails the row with an error code of the registry or `RENDER_FAILED`, a reopen met by an official print's register row (its guard, or a serialization failure on the period row) `PERIOD_NOT_CLOSED`) and `files-expire.job.ts` (`reports.files-expire`, hourly at :20: the jobs role finds ready files past their time, deleted and `expired`, and files queued over an hour, `failed` with `RENDER_LOST`). The worker depends on React and `@vertex-mizan/ui` only for the print layout (`dist/print`, built by `packages/ui`). `e2e/pdf.spec.ts` (`playwright.config.ts`, CI's Playwright image): a journal-book page of the longest values in the widest Arabic letters clips nothing and keeps its page count, the faces load, a long report flows over pages.
- `test/worker.ts` (`startWorker()`, `CapturingTransport`), `test/worker.test.ts` (the boot test: the real module on a fresh database installs pg-boss as the queue role, schedules every job once however many workers boot, and works a queued job to `completed`) and `test/jobs.test.ts` (the S01 jobs, run directly), `test/trust.test.ts` (S03's jobs, run directly: anchors and emails once, the archive opened with yauzl and checked against its manifest, a failed write, the expiry), `test/events.test.ts` (S05's jobs, run directly: a retry posting what an event freed, once; the sweep; the digest to owners and accountants, once a day), `test/reports.test.ts` (S09's jobs, run directly with a printer that keeps the HTML: a render ready for 24 hours and skipped the second time, an official print's register and audit rows with the same content hash on a reprint, its refusal after a reopen, a draft, a failure and the attempts limit, every report's render, the expiry and a lost file), `test/revenue.test.ts` (S06's release job with its clock: each ended month once, twice and at once, after a late invoice, a closed month left missing and released once reopened (S07), the slots of a company made before S06, the command; the owner role's URL only to remove those slots). `test/events.test.ts` also has S07's: a reopen's retry posting the event held in the month, once, and the digest's ended months not closed. A test's month is closed by `@vertex-mizan/db/testing`'s `closeMonthByHand()`. Files run one at a time (`vitest.config.ts`): the boot test's worker works the same database.

## Rules
- Queue names are `<module>.<name>` after the job's file; crons are UTC.
- Every job is idempotent: pg-boss retries, so running a job twice or concurrently leaves the same result.
- Job payloads carry ids, not records; a job loads fresh data in its own transaction.
- pg-boss runs as `mizan_queue` only, which reaches no `public` table. A job that reads company data gets the app role's database (one company, `withCompany`) or the audited `mizan_jobs` role's (cross-company, through its own policies; ADR 0003) with that job: add the module then, as Vertex Food's `jobs-database` module does. Never the owner role (ADR 0008).
- Accounting rules (releases, revaluation, settlement) are decided in `packages/ledger` and posted only through the posting function in `packages/db`; a job only triggers them.
- Explicit `@Inject(Token)` on constructor parameters, as in `apps/api`. Logs through the Nest `Logger`, never `console.log`; an error is logged through `describeForLog`, never its message.

## Tests
Run: `pnpm --filter @vertex-mizan/worker test` (needs the database URLs, `packages/db/CLAUDE.md`) · `pnpm --filter @vertex-mizan/worker typecheck`. `pnpm check:boot` starts the built API and worker together (CI).
