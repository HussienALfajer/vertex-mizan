# packages/scenarios

The scenario-book runner (ADR 0005, ADR 0008): it reads every case of `docs/scenarios/` and runs the `Approved` ones on a fresh test database, through what the system itself runs. The format is `docs/scenarios/README.md`; this package is its only reader. A private test workspace: nothing imports it and it has no build.

## Layout
- `src/format.ts`: the Zod schema of one case (`caseSchema`), strict objects in the README's snake_case, parsed into camelCase types (`ScenarioCase`, `Step`, `ExpectedEntry`, `ExpectedLine`, `ExpectedBalance`, `Outcome`). Currencies, amounts, rates and rate policies go through `@vertex-mizan/contracts`' schemas (`companyLedgersSchema`, `exchangeRateSchema`, `minorAmountSchema`). The cross checks: the currency shape against the book and parallel currencies, a parallel amount on every line and balance exactly when the company keeps a parallel ledger, every expected entry balanced in both ledgers, a ledger amount equal to the transaction amount in that ledger's own currency (ADR 0007), a third-currency case naming a third currency (in a row or a rate step), no decimal number in a payload, a reversal and a stated correction pointing at an expected entry, an inbox action (`retry`, `move`, `resolve`, `dismiss`; S05 rules 15–17; `recognise` and `split`, S06 rule 16) naming an earlier event step and a `resolve` an expected entry, a `revalue` of the parallel ledger only in a company that keeps one (S07), a `discard` naming an earlier `draft` step, an outcome's detail as one code with an optional value or several comma-separated (a failed checklist's items), the last step's outcome stated once (on the step or in `expect`), a correction at a rate step with a reason and no outcome, and an added account that fits its template (a postable code under one of its headers, not already in it, with a statement line of its class).
- `src/report-format.ts` (S09): `expectedReportSchema`, a case's `reports` (trial balance, account statement, income statement, balance sheet, profitability, journal book) with the checks a stated figure must pass whatever the system answers: the trial balance's pairs balance and its rows (with `non_zero` on) add up to the totals, a running balance runs, the income statement's subtotals are the sums of their categories' lines (`STATEMENT_LINES`), a balance-sheet line is the sum of its parts and the sheet balances with the year's result in equity, a contribution is revenue less direct costs and its margin `marginOf()` (one decimal, half away from zero), the journal book's pages run on and carry their totals forward, comparative figures only with a comparative, and (S10 rules 31–33, since `reports/opening-presentation.md`) the marks `opening_entry`, `before_books_start` and `pre_opening_result_in_equity` and a balance sheet's `year_result_pre_opening`, which `format.ts` allows only in a company with a books start and `src/reports.ts` compares since S10's PR 3; `format.ts` adds a parallel ledger only in a company that keeps one and named entries among the expected. `format.ts` also reads a company's `contracts` (code, kind, client) and the `service_department` step (S09 rule 34), and (S10) a company's `books_start` (the first day of a month, only with an `opening` step), the `opening` step (Hub's snapshot in short form with unique wallets, the accountant's lines with `book` and `parallel` stated only outside the ledger's own currency and the parallel only in a company that keeps one, the records' figures unique per slot, client and currency, and the register of prior documents, `prior_documents`, each listed once and dated before the books start, since `opening/before-books-start.md`) and `reverse_opening` (a reason), both run since S10's PR 1 (`src/opening.ts`); S11's `voucher` step (a cash voucher's draft, its header and lines in the payload, the executor's to read; read since `cash/transfer-and-exchange.md`, its executor arrives with S11's posting), the area `cash` and `expect.vouchers` (the exact set of the company's vouchers, each with its entry, a count's amounts and its cancelling reversal pointing at expected entries; since `cash/cash-count.md`, compared by `compare.ts`'s `compareVouchers` through the harness's `vouchers` reader, which arrives with S11's posting: a case stating vouchers without it fails); a manual line's `schedule` (an opening schedule by invoice and line, S10 rule 28; the manual payload is the executor's to read: `system.ts`'s `scheduleIdOf()` finds the latest opening's schedule through `hub.ts`'s `hubInvoiceLine()`) and the register's holds run since S10's PR 4.
- `src/book.ts`: `readBook()` (every `docs/scenarios/<area>/<rule>.md`, `README.md` left out, all errors collected), `yamlBlocks()` (every `` ```yaml `` block; any other fence, or an unclosed block, is a problem, so no case is hidden), `parseScenario()` (title, the one status line, every `yaml` block as a case, unique case names), `parseCase()` (YAML 1.2 core schema, integers as `bigint`, repeated keys refused).
- `src/harness.ts`: the plug-in points and `bookHarness`, the harness the book runs with: `setUp` (the case's company, ledgers, chart and the accounts a case adds to it, closed periods, bulletin calendar and rates), `executors` per step kind (`manual`, `reversal`, `rate`, `revalue`, `block`, `close`, `reopen`, `draft`, `discard`, `service_department`, `opening`, `reverse_opening`; `voucher` with S11), the `ledger` reader, the `reports` reader and the `vouchers` reader (S11). Hub's steps (`event`, `job`, the inbox actions, `recognise`, `split`) and the opening's snapshot and figures left with Hub (ADR 0021).
- `src/system.ts` (S03): what runs through the system. `setUp` makes the case's bookkeeper and company with `packages/db`'s services (`createCompanyBooks`, the case's chart through `applyChart()`, `createAccount` under the chart's header, `createDimensionValue` for each value the case names (S04), `setBulletinDaysOff`, `addBulletinHoliday`, `recordRate`) and closes its closed months through the close service; the `manual`, `reversal`, `rate`, `revalue`, `block`, `close`, `reopen`, `draft` and `discard` executors run through the posting function and the services the API uses; `ledger` reads the books as the reports read them. `src/agency-chart.ts`: the book's frozen copy of `service-agency@3` (`SERVICE_AGENCY_CHART`, `bookChart()`), which most approved cases name, since the product no longer offers it (ADR 0021). `src/clock.ts`: each case's clock (`advanceClock()`, `caseToday()`, `dayAfterMonth()`).
- `src/opening.ts` (S10): the `opening` executor (the draft saved through `saveOpening()` with the lines by code; the preview read and posted with its fingerprint by `postOpening()`; a refused post's failing checks as its detail) and `reverse_opening` (`reverseOpening()`).
- `src/compare.ts`: the exact-set comparison (`compareEntries`), `compareBalance` and `compareOpenItem`, as lists of readable differences.
- `src/run.ts`: `runCase()` (each step runs with its number in the context); `ScenarioFailure` carries every difference; `namedDimensionValues()`, the values a case names, which its setup makes (S04).
- `test/scenario-book.test.ts` with `test/global-setup.ts` (`createTestDatabase()` from `@vertex-mizan/db/testing`): the book itself, `vitest.scenarios.config.ts`. The runner's own tests are `src/*.test.ts` against a fake harness (`vitest.config.ts`, no database); `src/test-fixtures.ts` holds their synthetic cases.

## Rules
- **Proposed is skipped, never counted.** A `Proposed` case is an `it.skip` named `Proposed: <case>`, listed by the verbose reporter. Its file is still parsed: a broken file fails the run whatever its status.
- **An approved case needs every part.** Before anything runs, `runCase` checks the setup, the ledger reader and an executor for each step; anything missing fails the case. Never skip, stub or mock a missing part to make a case pass.
- **The exact set.** Entries are compared in posting order (source and date); the lines of an entry as a multiset, every field equal (account, dimensions, currency, transaction, book and parallel amounts); a missing or extra entry or line fails. Every posted entry has exactly one audit row. Stated balances match in the book, the parallel and, when stated, the transaction amount. A stated open item (ADR 0008) exists and matches in dimensions, currency and every remaining amount.
- **Outcomes.** Every step must be `done` when received, except a step that states its `outcome` (S05) and the last when `expect` states one: then it must be held or refused with exactly that reason, and that detail when stated (an unstated detail is not compared). A held step a later step frees posts then, and the entries show it where it posts. A stated correction's step returns the entries its answer lists (`usedBy`, posting order), which must be exactly the stated ones.
- **Through the real system.** Executors and readers get the run's database as the runtime roles (`ScenarioDatabase`: `app`, `jobs` through `createDatabase`), never the owner. A setup or an executor goes through the same services and the posting function the API and the worker use; the ledger reader reads the database, never an executor's return value.
- **Plugging in a part.** Register it in `bookHarness` (`src/harness.ts`) with the feature that brings it: the setup, the ledger reader and the `manual`, `reversal` and `rate` executors with S03, `event` and the inbox actions with S05, `job` with S07. A new step field or expected field goes into `docs/scenarios/README.md` and `src/format.ts` in the same PR, with a test in `src/book.test.ts`.
- Amounts are `bigint` minor units from end to end; no number amount and no float. Every test value is synthetic.

Run: `pnpm --filter @vertex-mizan/scenarios test` (the runner) · `pnpm test:scenarios` (the book; needs the local database, `packages/db/CLAUDE.md`).
