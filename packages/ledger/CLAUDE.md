# packages/ledger

The pure accounting mechanics of the double-entry core (ADR 0005, ADR 0007): journal lines with their transaction, book and parallel amounts, new measurements per ledger policy, carried amounts, balancing with rounding and exchange-difference lines, cumulative allocation and the integrity hash. No I/O: the posting function in `packages/db` (S03) reads rates and balances, calls these functions, and writes the ledger. Posting rules (which account, which event) come with their scenarios from S03 on (`/posting-rule`); nothing here decides an account.

## Layout
- `src/<module>.ts` with `src/<module>.test.ts` next to it; `src/index.ts` re-exports every public module.
- `lines.ts`: `Line` (kinds `measured`, `carried`, `difference`, `rounding`; `item`, the `ItemRef` of a line on a control account: the item's id and its movement kind, S04), `CarriedAmounts`, `Conversion` (the rate a ledger amount was converted at, `policy` or `transaction`), `carriedLine()`, `negate()`, `keptLedgers()`, `ledgerCurrency()`, `ledgerAmount()`.
- `rates.ts`: `PublicationCalendar`, `lastPublicationDay()` (*P*, the latest day on or before a date that is neither a weekly day off nor a holiday), `rateInForce()` (the newest row of a type effective on or before the date, among rows of one date the one recorded last; a `bulletin` row only if effective on or after *P*, ADR 0011) and `policyRate()` (direct, or a cross through `via`, each quote under the same rule).
- `chart.ts`: `checkLineAgainstChart()` (S02 rule 8), what S03 calls for every line: the account known, postable, not archived or blocked; the item a line names (S04 rules 5 and 9: `OPEN_ITEM_REQUIRED` on a `document` control account, `ACCOUNT_NOT_CONTROL` elsewhere, none on a `running` account, whose item is the client's; `ACCOUNT_IS_CONTROL` is no longer raised); the transaction currency equal to the account's restriction; one value per dimension type and every required type present; no archived value on a manual line (a Hub line keeps Hub's records); a contract's client equal to the line's client. A reversal's line (origin `reversal`) carries a posted line, so only its account's state is checked again.
- `measure.ts`, **the pattern to copy for a mechanic with rules**: `measure()` and `measuredLine()` for new measurements.
- `carried.ts`: `clearOpenItem()` (in proportion to what remains, from the item's carrying amount, ADR 0013; the last clearing clears everything left), `moveRunningBalance()` (average carrying amount, zero-crossing split), `reverseLines()` (each line keeps its item).
- `items.ts` (S04, ADR 0013): `ItemState` (an item as the posting function reads its balance row), `emptyBalance()`, `moveItem()` (a document item opened by a new measurement, cleared toward zero, `OPEN_ITEM_DIRECTION`, `RELEASE_EXCEEDS_BALANCE`; a running item's `add`, `clear` at its average and the split at zero), `checkItemLine()` (`OPEN_ITEM_MISMATCH`; `OPEN_ITEM_MEMO_REQUIRED` and `adjusted` on a Hub or opening item, rules 7–8), `checkDueDate()` (rule 6), `runningVoidDifference()` and `runningVoidDifferenceLines()` (rule 13: after a reversal against a running item's balance or across zero, the difference to the carrying amount of a new movement: against the balance a difference line on the item, across zero the item cleared and the excess measured anew with its rate (owner, 2026-11-06); then the realised gain or loss; none with the balance or onto zero), `movementOn()`. `manualReversalLines()` refuses the entry that follows a reversal (`followsReversal`).
- `ageing.ts` (S04 rules 14, 16 and 17): `dueDateInForce()` (the newest row effective on or before a date, the document date with none), `ageingOf()` (each open item's days past due and bucket, totals per client, per bucket in the ledger's currency and per transaction currency) and `fundTotals()` (funds held and receivables apart). `items.ts` also has `itemPositions()` (each item an entry's lines move, before and after, for the preview); `integrity-check.ts` has `openItemProblems()` (each control account against its items' movements per currency and ledger, each item's balance row against its movements); `periods.ts` has `daysBetween()` (calendar days).
- `allocation.ts`: `allocate()` and `allocateCarried()` (cumulative rounding, each ledger separately).
- `periods.ts`: `periodOf()` (the calendar month of a date), `lastDayOfMonth()`, `fiscalYearOf()` (the fiscal year of a date for a start month, named after the calendar year it starts in; entry numbers restart each one, ADR 0012).

## Rules
- **Amounts** are `bigint` minor units. Conversions go through contracts' `convert()` only, and proportions through `share()`: both round half away from zero once, on the exact value. Never a float, never a `number` amount.
- **New measurement or carried amount (ADR 0007).** A line that measures something new converts per ledger with `measure()`. A line that releases, clears, settles or reverses a balance carries that balance's amounts (`clearOpenItem`, `moveRunningBalance`, `allocateCarried`, `releaseParts`, `reverseLines`) and is never converted again.
- **Rates.** A ledger uses the transaction's own rate (or a manual entry's dealt rate for the pair, S03 rule 13) only when its policy is `market`, accepts it, and the rate quotes the pair to convert; otherwise its policy's rate type in force on the entry date: the more recent of the direct rate and a cross through the company's other ledger currency (the book currency for the parallel ledger, the parallel currency for the book ledger), a cross being as recent as its older quote and the direct rate winning a tie. A `bulletin` is in force only from the latest publication day on or before the entry date (the company's `PublicationCalendar`, ADR 0011): a working day needs its own bulletin, a day off or a holiday takes the last published one. A missing rate throws `RATE_MISSING`: never a default, never another type, never yesterday's bulletin on a working day.
- **Balance.** Every entry goes through `balanceEntry()` last. It adds a rounding line only when the exact, unrounded values balance (contracts' `sumsToZeroExactly`), so the difference is conversion rounding (at most half a minor unit per converted line); rounding lines passed in (a reversal's) must balance their ledger by themselves. Any other residual is the posting rule's to post first, as difference lines (`exchangeDifferenceLines`); otherwise the entry is refused with `ENTRY_UNBALANCED`.
- **No difference explains an imbalance in one currency.** When every line but difference and rounding lines is in one transaction currency, those lines balance in it, or `exchangeDifferenceLines()` and `balanceEntry()` refuse them (`ENTRY_UNBALANCED`): an overpayment is a liability to post, never a gain. When they are also all new measurements, no difference line is allowed: their residual can only be rounding.
- **Difference and rounding lines** carry an amount in one ledger only: in the ledger currency, or on an item in another currency with a transaction amount of zero.
- **Measured lines are built, never taken.** `balanceEntry()` checks that a measured amount is the conversion of its transaction amount at the rate it records, not that the rate is the one the policy picks. So the posting function builds every new measurement with `measuredLine()` and every reversal with `reverseLines()`, and never accepts lines from its caller.
- **One method per balance.** An over-time deferral (S06) is split with `releaseParts()` over `overTimeMonths()`, never with `allocateCarried()`: its transaction amount is cut by days from the period's start and its other ledgers over the days not elapsed (option A, owner 2026-11-10). A cancelled project's remainder is split with `cancelledProjectSplit()`. A credit takes its part with `creditParts()`, and from then on the schedule's months not released are cut from what remains with `remainingReleaseParts()`; a credit's void splits what it gives back with `voidedCreditParts()` (owner, 2026-11-10). Any other schedule known in advance is split with `allocateCarried()`; movements as they come are cleared with `clearOpenItem()` or `moveRunningBalance()` (through `moveItem()` for an open item). The methods round differently, so one balance never mixes them.
- **Purity** is tested (`purity.test.ts`): source files import only `@vertex-mizan/contracts` and their siblings (`integrity.ts` also `node:crypto`), and use no clock, randomness, timers, network or `process`.
- **Errors.** A business refusal is a `DomainError` with a code from contracts' registry (a new code goes there first). A line whose amounts contradict its kind, or an argument no caller should pass, is a programming error: `TypeError` or `RangeError`.
- **Integrity.** `canonicalJson()` and `entryHash()` must never change once an entry is posted: a stored chain is verified with them for at least ten years. The pinned hashes in `integrity.test.ts` (of `entryHash()` and of a stored entry through `chainEntryValue()`) fail on any change. A `bigint` and its decimal string serialise differently, so the posting function and the integrity check build the entry value from the same typed fields (node-postgres returns `bigint` columns as strings: parse them back first).
- Arithmetic on `*Minor` fields is allowed here (`packages/config/biome-plugins/minor-arithmetic.grit`). Every test value is synthetic; account codes are the scenario book's placeholders.

Changing an exported type or function changes `packages/db` and every posting rule: run `pnpm typecheck` at the root.

Run: `pnpm --filter @vertex-mizan/ledger test`.
